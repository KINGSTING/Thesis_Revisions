\chapter{Review of Related Literature}

This chapter synthesized the body of scholarly work that formed the foundation for this research, which aimed to optimize solid waste management (SWM) policies for a Philippine Local Government Unit (LGU) using an Agent-Based Modeling (ABM) and Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) approach. The review was organized thematically. It began by examining the specific context of SWM in the Philippines under the Ecological Solid Waste Management Act \citep{RA9003}. It then explored the theoretical underpinnings of pro-environmental behavior and the policy instruments used to influence it. Subsequently, it delved into the computational methodologies of ABM and DRL, highlighting their applications in environmental management and their synergistic potential. The chapter concluded by synthesizing these areas to clearly identify the research gap that this thesis aimed to address.
