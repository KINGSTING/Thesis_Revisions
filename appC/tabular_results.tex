\chapter{Tables of Result}
\label{ch:appendix_c}

This appendix provides a comprehensive tabulation of the simulation data generated across the four experimental scenarios: the Baseline (Status Quo), Pure Enforcement, Pure Incentives, and the proposed HuDRL PPO approach. These tables serve as the granular evidentiary basis for the comparative analysis and performance evaluation presented in the main chapters. By documenting the raw quarterly metrics for each of the seven barangays in the Municipality of Bacolod, this section allows for a detailed verification of the specific financial allocations and behavioral outcomes that emerged under different policy regimes over the three-year simulation period.

The data is organized sequentially by scenario, with individual subsections dedicated to each barangay to facilitate localized trend analysis. For every quarter (Q1 through Q12), the tables detail the financial dynamics, including the Total Budget, Local Government Unit (LGU) Allocation, and the specific distribution of funds toward Information, Education, and Communication (IEC), Enforcement, and Incentives. Furthermore, key performance indicators such as the resulting Household Compliance Rate and the count of Active Enforcers are recorded, providing a complete picture of the resource-to-outcome dynamics within the Agent-Based Model.
